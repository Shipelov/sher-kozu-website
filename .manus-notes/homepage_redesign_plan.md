# Homepage Redesign Plan

## Current Structure (sections in order):
1. Hero — big image, headline "Персональное фермерство", subtitle, 2 CTAs
2. "Как это работает" — 3 steps (choose animal, follow farm life, get products)
3. "Животное недели" — featured animal card with photo
4. Animal Gallery — grid of animal cards from API
5. "Почему Шерь Козу" — 4 value cards (emotional connection, transparency, premium products, closed club)
6. Partner Program — MASSIVE section: full CRM form with drag-drop attachments, progress bar, FAQ, Bitrix24 status, "What admin sees"
7. Ecosystem Routes — internal product navigation cards (Dashboard, Tracker, Club, Profile)
8. Atmosphere + AI-curator — brand atmosphere card + "next layer" AI card
9. Final CTA — green banner with 4 navigation buttons

## Problems Identified:
- No concept explanation — assumes visitor knows "personal farming"
- "Animal of the week" too early — user hasn't understood the model yet
- Partner program takes ~40% of page — B2B content on B2C landing
- "Ecosystem routes" and "AI-curator" — internal product language, not marketing
- Too many CTAs competing for attention
- No social proof (testimonials, numbers)
- No "who is this for" section

## New Structure (approved):
1. **Hero** — keep but refine headline to explain concept immediately
2. **What is personal farming** — NEW section explaining the concept clearly
3. **How it works** — keep 3 steps, refine copy
4. **Who is this for** — NEW section: families, natural food lovers, gift givers + collapsed partner link
5. **Animal Gallery** — keep as is (move "animal of the week" to /animals page)
6. **Why us** — keep 4 cards + add numbers/facts
7. **Social proof** — NEW section: key metrics or testimonials
8. **Final CTA** — 1 clear action: "Choose an animal" + secondary "Learn more"

## What to REMOVE from homepage:
- "Animal of the week" → move to /animals
- Partner program → collapse to link "For partners" in footer → separate /partners page
- "Ecosystem routes" → remove (internal language)
- "AI-curator / next layer" → remove (roadmap, not marketing)
- "What admin sees" → remove (admin content)
