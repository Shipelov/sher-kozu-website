# Homepage Marketing Audit — 21 March 2026

## Current Structure (top to bottom):
1. Navbar
2. Hero — big image, headline "Персональное фермерство", subtitle, 2 CTAs (Выбрать животное / Как это работает)
3. "Как это устроено" — 3 steps (choose animal, follow farm life, get products)
4. "Почему именно мы" — 4 value cards (emotional bond, transparency, premium products, club)
5. Animal Gallery — live animal cards with names, breeds, photos, prices
6. "Животное недели" — featured animal spotlight
7. Partner Program — HUGE section with full form, drag-drop attachments, FAQ, CRM status, admin info
8. Ecosystem Routes — links to Dashboard, Tracker, Club, Profile
9. Atmosphere/Brand block — family farm image, AI future teaser
10. Final CTA — "Choose your scenario" with 4 buttons

## Problems Identified:
1. **Information overload** — too many sections compete for attention, no clear narrative arc
2. **"Животное недели" too early** — user hasn't understood the concept yet, seeing a specific animal is premature
3. **Partner Program dominates** — takes up ~40% of page real estate with full CRM form, FAQ, attachment upload, admin info. This is B2B content on a B2C landing page
4. **No clear "What is personal farming?" explanation** — hero assumes user already knows
5. **Ecosystem routes section** — reads like internal documentation, not marketing copy
6. **AI/future teaser** — confusing for first-time visitor, internal roadmap language
7. **Too many CTAs competing** — dilutes conversion focus
8. **Missing social proof** — no testimonials, no numbers about existing members
9. **Missing "For whom" section** — who is this for? families? foodies? gift-givers?

## Redesign Proposal — Narrative Arc:
1. HERO — Emotional hook + clear value prop "What is personal farming?"
2. PROBLEM/SOLUTION — Why regular dairy is broken, what we offer instead
3. HOW IT WORKS — 3 simple steps (keep existing, refine copy)
4. FOR WHOM — 3-4 audience segments (families, foodies, gift-givers, conscious consumers)
5. ANIMAL GALLERY — Browse real animals (keep existing)
6. VALUES — Trust signals (transparency, premium, club) — condensed
7. SOCIAL PROOF — Testimonials or numbers
8. FINAL CTA — Clear single conversion action
9. FOOTER NOTE — "Для партнёров" as a small link/button, not a full section

## Key Decisions:
- Remove "Животное недели" from homepage (move to /animals page)
- Collapse partner program to a single "Для партнёров" button → separate /partners page
- Remove ecosystem routes section (internal language)
- Remove AI/future teaser (internal roadmap)
- Add "For whom" section
- Add social proof section
- Simplify final CTA to one clear action
- Tighten hero copy to explain the concept in 1 sentence
